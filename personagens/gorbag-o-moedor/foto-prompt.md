# Prompt de Imagem — Gorbag, o Moedor

Digital illustration, dark fantasy character concept art, gritty linework with muted, grimy coloring, in the style of a hand-drawn RPG character portrait. A colossal male ogre, 2.45 m tall, about 340 kg, 34 years old, with a huge protruding belly and arms as thick as tree trunks. His skin is grey-green, thick and leathery, covered in old blade scars and burn marks. Wide heavy jaw, a crooked lopsided grin missing several teeth, small predatory eyes sizing up the viewer like a cut of meat. He wears ragged leather trousers, steel-toed boots bound with chains, and a filthy butcher's apron that is comically tiny for his size, printed with faded little flowers and stained with dried and fresh blood. Several iron meat hooks hang from his belt, dripping blood, and a small bone sticks out of his mouth like a toothpick. Resting on his shoulder is an enormous two-handed butcher's cleaver, as large as a human torso, its edge freshly sharpened. Menacing, cynical, professional butcher posture. Plain neutral background, character reference sheet pose, portrait framing from the waist up, centered.
