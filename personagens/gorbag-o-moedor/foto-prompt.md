# Prompt de Imagem — Gorbag, o Moedor

Dark fantasy digital illustration, character concept art, detailed painterly rendering. A colossal ogre butcher, 2.45 m tall and about 340 kg, 34 years old: massive pot belly, arms as thick as tree trunks, gray-green thick hide covered in blade scars and old burns, wide jaw, crooked grin with several missing teeth, a bald scarred head and a small hoop earring, a gaze that is always choosing the cut. He wears ragged leather trousers, steel-toed boots wrapped in chains, a tiny stained butcher's apron far too small for him (faded little flowers under dried blood) and iron meat hooks hanging from a heavy belt. A huge two-handed cleaver the size of a man's torso rests on his shoulder, rusty and dripping blood. Full-body character reference pose, centered, plain neutral stormy background.
