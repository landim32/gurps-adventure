# Prompt de Imagem — Sae'Varyn

Digital illustration, dark fantasy character concept art, clean linework with soft muted coloring, in the style of a hand-drawn RPG character portrait, grim and melancholic tone. A tall, slender dark elf man of indeterminate great age, with sharply pointed long ears, ashen grey skin, high cold cheekbones, and long white hair worn in tight braids; pale ice-coloured eyes with a calm, bitter, questioning gaze. He is a fallen warrior-priest: he wears a white Templar surcoat, now stained, torn and patched, with the red cross ripped away so only the ghost of its shape remains against the cloth, over a light leather jerkin. A heavy flanged mace hangs at his belt and a small round buckler rests on his left arm; he carries a dark necromancer's staff, and a rosary of black beads hangs at his side, which one grey hand touches at the chest as if by habit. Around him hints of his craft: a surgeon's roll of instruments and a pouch of bone and salt. His expression is serene and sorrowful, coldly aristocratic, a man who tends the dead with the same careful hands that once healed the living. Plain neutral dark background, character reference sheet pose, portrait framing from the waist up, centered.

*Estilo:* desenho/ilustração (não fotorrealista).
