# Prompt de Imagem — Kagehira

Digital illustration, fantasy character concept art, clean linework with soft coloring, in the
style of a hand-drawn RPG character portrait, restrained and sombre palette. Subject: a human
man of the Far-Eastern Sahud kingdoms, 1,78 m tall, 70,4 kg, 36 years old — lean and wiry
rather than muscular, narrow shoulders, hard forearms, close-cropped black hair at the jaw,
clean-shaven flat face, lowered watchful eyes, calloused hands with scraped nails. He wears
unremarkable travel clothes of a middle-class caravan guest: a muted indigo wrap robe with
trousers tucked into soft sandals, a plain wide-brimmed travel cloak, a cloth sash; no
armor, no heraldry, nothing that marks him as anyone in particular. A long slightly curved
single-edged sword rides at his hip on a silk cord, its plain scabbard scuffed; a coiled
silk rope hangs at his back, a small lacquered box of glass vials and a length of cord are
tucked in the sash. Expression and pose: utterly still, weight already shifted toward the
exit, face politely empty — the stillness of a man who has measured the room twice. Plain
neutral background, character reference sheet pose, portrait framing from the waist up,
centered.

*Estilo:* desenho/ilustração (não fotorrealista).
