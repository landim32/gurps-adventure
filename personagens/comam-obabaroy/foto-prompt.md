# Prompt de Imagem — Comam Obabaroy

Digital illustration, fantasy character concept art, clean linework with warm soft coloring,
in the style of a hand-drawn RPG character portrait. A middle-aged Middle-Eastern-looking
mercenary man with a receding hairline, the rest of his long dark hair tied back in a
ponytail, and a neat goatee beard. He is missing one eye and wears a simple brown leather
eyepatch over it; his remaining eye and his whole face carry a wide, confident, mischievous
grin, as if he just told a joke. Sturdy, athletic build. He wears a full set of hardened
brown leather armor: a leather cap, a studded leather cuirass over the torso, leather bracers
on the arms, open-palm leather gauntlets, leather greaves on the legs and tall laced boots.
A curved scimitar hangs at his hip alongside two small war picks tucked into his belt, and a
heavy oversized steel crossbow is slung across his back with a goat's-foot cocking lever
hanging beside it. Standing pose, weight relaxed on one leg, one hand resting near the
scimitar's hilt, exuding easygoing swagger rather than tension. Plain neutral background,
character reference sheet pose, portrait framing from the waist up, centered.

*Estilo:* desenho/ilustração (não fotorrealista).
