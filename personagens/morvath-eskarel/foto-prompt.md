# Prompt de Imagem — Morvath Eskarel, o Lich

Digital illustration, dark fantasy character concept art, clean linework with soft muted coloring, in the style of a hand-drawn RPG character portrait. In the foreground, an old, sickly human necromancer, 71 years old, 1.68 m tall and thin at about 61 kg, standing hunched: mostly bald on top with a few long wisps of thin white hair at the sides, pale waxy skin with gray undertones, sunken cheeks. He has suffered a stroke: the left half of his face droops, with a sagging eyelid, a slack mouth and a downturned corner of the lips, while the right half stays tense and distrustful. He wears rich, high-class robes: a long dark velvet robe with embroidered gold trim, a high stiff collar, a heavy fur-lined cloak and jeweled rings on his thin fingers, all well kept but hanging loosely on his wasted body. With his good hand he clutches against his chest, with great care and in a protective, almost loving grip, a dark glass bottle sealed with wax, its surface densely encrusted with small glowing precious gems of different colors; he angles his body to shield it, as if afraid someone will take it. On his paralyzed side, the left, he leans on a plain wooden staff used as a cane, his weak hand hanging limp over its top. Behind him, in a dim gothic study, two figures stand in the background: on one side a silent zombie servant in a tattered servant's livery, gray decaying skin, blank eyes, hands clasped in front of him; on the other side a faceless puppet warrior, a stitched cloth-and-wood marionette with a completely smooth blank face and visible strings, holding a sword and a round shield. Cold bluish rim light on the old man, faint pale glow leaking from the bottle, muted dark palette. Full-body three-quarter view, the old man centered in front and the two background figures smaller behind him, one on each side.
