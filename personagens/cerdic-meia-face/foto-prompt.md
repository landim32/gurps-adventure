# Prompt de Imagem — Cerdic Meia-Face

Dark fantasy digital illustration, character concept art with clean linework and moody, warm-lit coloring, in the style of a hand-drawn RPG character portrait; a gaunt, emaciated human man of about fifty, unnaturally thin with narrow shoulders and bony wrists, deeply sunken cheeks, weathered lined skin and a scholar's sharp, intelligent eyes; the entire left half of his face is covered in old, glossy, ridged burn scar tissue running from his temple down to the corner of his mouth, decades healed, and the scarring climbs over his skull so that a quarter of his scalp is bald and shining, while the unburned right side has long, unkempt dark hair streaked with grey that he combs uselessly across the damage; his left eyelid does not fully close and only half his face moves, giving him a crooked permanent half-smile; he wears a threadbare cloth cap pushed back on his head, a patched commoner's tunic and a long soot-stained hooded cloak over a light fitted leather cuirass, hands bare and stained with ash and charcoal, a battered hand-copied grimoire hanging in a small satchel at his hip and a small glowing red gemstone strung on a cord around his neck; he cradles a small flame floating just above his open palm and gazes down at it with tender, unsettling affection, lips parted as if speaking to it; warm firelight from the flame lights his scarred side from below; plain neutral dark background, character reference sheet pose, portrait framing from the waist up, centered.
