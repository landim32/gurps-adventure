# Prompt de Imagem — Irmão Kaelric

Digital illustration, fantasy character concept art, clean linework with soft muted coloring, in the style of a hand-drawn RPG character portrait, medieval realism. A towering young human man, a gentle giant well over two meters tall with a broad-shouldered, powerful build and huge hands — clearly the tallest man in any room. He has the look of a christianized viking: brown hair and a full brown beard worn in braids, a calm but tired gaze, serene and questioning. He wears a knight-monk's outfit that blends Templar and northern styles: a white surcoat bearing a red cross over a riveted chain mail hauberk, a mail coif around his head and shoulders, leather vambraces on the forearms, leather gloves, and laced leather boots. At his belt hangs a heavy flanged mace; a rosary and a small vial of holy oil dangle at his side. He holds a large heater shield marked with the red Templar cross, its size looking almost normal against his huge frame. His expression is kind and thoughtful, one open hand raised slightly as if offering a blessing rather than a threat. Plain neutral background, character reference sheet pose, portrait framing from the waist up, centered, with a sense of his imposing height.

*Estilo:* desenho/ilustração (não fotorrealista).
