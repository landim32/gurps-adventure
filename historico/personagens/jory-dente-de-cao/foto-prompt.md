## Forma humana

Dark fantasy digital illustration, character concept art, clean linework with soft coloring, in the style of a hand-drawn RPG character portrait, gritty and street-level. Full-body shot from head to toe, feet visible, character reference sheet pose, centered, plain light background (white or pale gray).
